Retreat Player plays MP3s of dhamma talks, bells, guided meditations and chanting during a meditation retreat. Its one principle: once playback starts, nothing interrupts it.

- Every recording is downloaded into app-private storage when it is loaded, so playback needs no network — airplane mode is fine.
- Playback runs in a foreground service holding a wake lock: the OS cannot kill or pause it, even with the screen off for an hour-long talk.
- The app takes no audio focus, so nothing else on the phone can duck or pause a talk.
- While a recording plays the screen stays on, showing the progress the whole time.
- A recording plays only because you pressed play, and only once: when it ends, nothing else starts automatically.

Load recordings from files on the phone, a shared kDrive folder (Infomaniak public share link) or any podcast feed with audio enclosures. File each recording under Bells & chanting, Guided meditations or Dharma talks and order them as you like. The bottom player shows elapsed and remaining time side by side, with play/pause, stop, ±10 s and back-to-beginning.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Network is used only while loading recordings; playback is fully offline.
