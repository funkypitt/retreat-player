# Privacy policy — Retreat Player

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Retreat Player (`com.freedomfighter.retreatplayer`) is published by Pierre Gallaz. It plays the recordings you load. It is free, has no ads, no account and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library.

## Network use

The network is used only when you ask the app to load recordings:

- from a public kDrive share link you paste (requests go to kdrive.infomaniak.com);
- from a podcast feed address you paste (requests go to that feed's server and to the audio files it lists).

Each request is a plain download started by your tap. No identifier, account or device information is sent. Nothing is contacted at any other time.

## Data kept on the phone

Your settings, your schedule or library, and the audio files you load stay in the app's private storage on the phone. They are deleted when the app is uninstalled. Android's own backup may copy the settings (not the audio files) to the Google account configured on the phone; that is controlled by the phone's backup settings, not by the app.

## Permissions

The app asks only for what its job needs: playing audio through a foreground service, keeping the processor awake while it plays, showing a notification with playback controls, and reading the audio files you pick. 

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
